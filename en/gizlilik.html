2. Data Processed and Purposes

a) Site Usage Statistics

Our website uses Google Analytics cookies to measure and improve the quality of our service. These cookies collect anonymous statistical data such as pages visited, session duration and general usage trends. This data is not used to identify individuals.

b) Anonymous Visitor Counter

Our site displays a live visitor counter. This counter works with a random session ID that contains no personal data. The session ID is deleted when the browser is closed; no name, e-mail address or IP address is recorded.

c) Contact Form

The name, e-mail address and message content you submit through the contact form on our site are processed solely to respond to your request. Form data is transmitted via the Formspree infrastructure and is not shared with third parties.

d) WhatsApp and Telegram Communication

When you contact us via WhatsApp or Telegram, the privacy policies and terms of use of those platforms apply. Information you share through these platforms is subject to the data processing rules of the relevant platform.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Your personal data is not sold, not marketed to third parties and is not shared for commercial purposes.

Data is shared only with the infrastructure providers technically required for the service to operate:

  • Google — Site analytics and performance measurement
  • Formspree — Contact form infrastructure
  • Supabase — Visitor counter database (anonymous)
  • Netlify — Website hosting infrastructure

These infrastructure providers act within the framework of their own privacy policies.

4. Your Rights Under KVKK

Under Article 11 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, you have the following rights:

  • 🔍
    Right to information: To learn whether your personal data is being processed and the purpose of processing.
  • ✏️
    Rectification: To request correction if your personal data has been processed incompletely or incorrectly.
  • 🗑️
    Erasure: To request deletion or destruction of your data when the reasons requiring its processing no longer exist.
  • 🚫
    Objection: To object to a result against you arising from the analysis of processed data exclusively by automated systems.
  • 💰
    Compensation: To claim compensation for damages suffered due to unlawful processing of data.

To exercise these rights, you may apply to info@kilmanbilisim.com. Your applications will be answered within 30 days at the latest.
