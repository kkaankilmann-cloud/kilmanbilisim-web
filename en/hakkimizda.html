Why We Were Founded

Small and medium-sized businesses struggle to access the software and automation tools used by large corporations. License costs are high, setup times are long, and technical support is limited. Kılman Bilişim was founded to close this gap.

Our goal is to make AI and automation technologies understandable, accessible, and scalable. Whether your team has two people or fifty, everyone should have access to the same quality solutions.

What We Do

We provide AI-powered automation systems, custom software development, and web and mobile solutions. Every project is designed according to the client's existing workflow — we don't sell ready-made packages.

Our own products serve as concrete references: a multi-tenant Business Management System (revenue/expense/customer/product management, PWA-supported), this corporate website with 316 pages in 9 languages, and a real estate automation system running on a 62-node AI agent chain.

Who We Serve

We focus on SMEs in real estate, healthcare, logistics, manufacturing, retail, and service sectors. While based in Maltepe, Istanbul, we manage all projects remotely, serving clients across Turkey and internationally.

Any business looking to automate repetitive tasks, strengthen customer communication with AI, or integrate existing systems is the right fit for us.

How We Work

We start by listening to your current workflow, evaluating which steps are suitable for automation, then build on top of your existing systems without disrupting them. Every deliverable goes through triple-layer verification, and we share what was done and why through transparent reporting.

For our detailed methodology, visit our How We Work page.
