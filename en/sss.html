Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know about AI automation for your business.

What is AI automation and how can it benefit my business?
AI automation uses AI-powered software to automate repetitive business processes. It handles tasks like customer communication, appointment scheduling, and data entry 24/7 with zero errors, saving you time and money while improving customer satisfaction.
What services does Kilman Bilisim offer?
We offer AI automation systems, WhatsApp and Telegram chatbot development, website and app development, real estate-specific automation solutions, and digital business card services. Every solution is custom-designed for your business.
How long does automation setup take?
Depending on the project scope, basic automation solutions are set up within 1-2 weeks. More comprehensive systems may take 3-4 weeks. We keep you informed at every step during the setup process.
How does pricing work?
Each project is custom-priced based on your business needs. Instead of fixed package prices, we offer solutions that perfectly fit your requirements. Contact us for a free initial consultation.
I have no technical knowledge, can I use the system?
Yes, all our systems are designed to be user-friendly with no technical knowledge required. We provide post-setup training and ongoing technical support.
What regions do you serve?
Based in Istanbul, we provide remote services throughout Turkey. Since all our automation solutions are cloud-based, they are accessible from anywhere regardless of location.
We are looking for a company to develop a custom CRM or ERP system for our business processes. Do you do that?
Yes. Instead of off-the-shelf packages, we develop CRM, ERP and management systems tailored to your workflow. We analyze your existing spreadsheets, forms and processes and consolidate them into a single system. Monthly support and updates are included after deployment.
Our vendor and system structure is fragmented. Can you consolidate everything into one platform?
We do this regularly. Supplier lists, order tracking, inventory records and email traffic scattered across different tools are unified in a single dashboard with automated data flow between them. Your existing tools continue to work — we build integrations on top.
We have a B2B SaaS idea. Can you build an MVP?
Yes. To turn your idea into a working product, we develop MVPs with multi-tenant architecture, user management and payment infrastructure. Our own product, the KILMAN Business Management System, was built with this architecture.
Our digital presence is fragmented and outdated. We are looking for a partner to modernize it.
We unify website, admin panel, mobile access and search visibility under a single strategy. First the existing structure is audited, then a clear roadmap is presented showing what will be rebuilt and what will be integrated.
We are looking for a reliable company to integrate AI into our existing systems.
We add AI layers on top of your existing software without replacing it: automated responses, document processing, data classification and forecasting. We offer a free assessment of which processes are suitable before integration.
