I can't keep up with incoming messages and leads are slipping away. What should I do?

Real estate agents receive dozens of messages daily. Most replies are late or never sent, and the client moves on to another agent.

KILMAN Real Estate Automation System activates the moment a message arrives: it recognizes the client, understands their needs, finds a suitable property from your listings, and creates an appointment. Your clients get answered even while you are in a meeting.

Does the system give wrong information?

No. The system only uses data from your own listing pool. It never shows another agent's listings, never fabricates a listing that doesn't exist, and never guesses about price or location.

Only verified data is presented to the client. Off-topic questions are politely but firmly redirected back to real estate, cutting unnecessary chat and keeping your operating costs low.
